Description

Two bedrooms around an open kitchen and living room

The COR Model B1 centers on an open kitchen and living room with a 6-foot island, with a bedroom on each side. The primary bedroom has a wide closet and sits next to the bathroom, which has a 36" × 72" walk-in shower and a 54" vanity. Bedroom 2 has its own closet, and a stacked washer and dryer closet opens off the kitchen. A covered porch frames the front entry, and a kitchen door opens onto a covered back patio.

It's an all-electric home: a mini-split heat pump for heating and cooling, an electric water heater, and a solar PV system are part of the build.
